# Terms — house list

Parsed by `detect.py`. Sections are load-bearing; the header text routes the
entry. A project keeps its own copy at
`.claude/skills/unpack-jargon/terms.md`, and its entries overlay this one.

Records here are audience-independent: things always worth a second look, and
words that always look like jargon to a dictionary while carrying no load.

## Domain terms

Always raise these as candidates, whatever the dictionary says. Add a project's
own vocabulary to the project file, not here.

## Decided - keep and gloss

A term the reader needs in its precise form, with the gloss to use at first use.
Format `- term: the gloss`.

## Decided - replace

A term with a plain equivalent that loses nothing. Format `- term: replacement`.

## Decided - fine as-is

Terms a given audience reads without help. These stop being reported. This is
what makes the second run on a project quieter than the first.
